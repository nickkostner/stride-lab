Filming guide
  1. Phone in landscape, on a tripod or shelf at hip height.
  2. Stand it 3–4 m away, square to the side of the runner.
  3. Whole body in frame, head to feet, the whole time. A treadmill is ideal.
  4. Run at a steady pace for at least 6 strides before you start the clip.
  5. Fitted clothes and good light help the pose model see your joints.

How Ochy works

Ochy is a running-form app from Rennes, France, founded in 2021. You film about 10 seconds of yourself running, and it returns joint angles, a score and drills in under a minute. Tags show how sure I am: CERTAIN means a public source says it, LIKELY is a strong inference, and GUESS fills a gap.

What the user gets

  • CERTAINRecord with a phone, no wearables. Results in under 60 s from about a 10 s clip, side or back view, on road, track or treadmill.
  • CERTAINFeedback on foot strike, posture, knee alignment, arm carriage, head position, cadence and pelvic stability, plus exercises for each problem.
  • CERTAINStrict filming rules (clip length, framing). Reviewers say following them makes the difference.
  • LIKELYAbout 15 metrics, including cadence and ground contact time. That figure comes from a competitor's page.
  • CERTAINFree download with a paywall. Part of the analysis is greyed out until you pay.

The pipeline behind it

LIKELYEvery app in this category runs the same five stages. Ochy's value is in tuning stages 3–5 and in the filming rules that keep stage 1 clean.

  1. LIKELYPose estimation. A neural network finds the joints in each frame. Off-the-shelf models (MediaPipe, MoveNet, RTMPose) are good enough. Ochy says it uses its own computer-vision AI GUESS on cloud GPUs, given the upload-then-wait flow.
  2. CERTAINClean-up. In side view the far leg is hidden, so models swap left and right legs. You have to fix that, or every per-leg number is wrong. This clone checks which labelling keeps each leg continuous from the previous frame.
  3. CERTAINGait events. Initial contact is the frame where a foot reaches ground level. Step rhythm comes from the pelvis bouncing once per step.
  4. CERTAINMetrics. Shin angle, knee bend and foot angle at landing; how far the foot lands ahead of the hips; trunk lean; vertical oscillation; ground contact time. Pixels become centimetres by scaling to your height.
  5. LIKELYRules and coaching. Each metric gets good, watch or fix against a target range, and each flag maps to a fixed drill list. A language model may write the prose GUESS, but the judgement is rules.

What it would take to compete

  • CERTAINThe algorithm is not the moat. Everything on the Analyse tab took a few hundred lines on top of a free model.
  • LIKELYThe moat is validation data (clips paired with lab motion capture, to prove accuracy) and distribution deals like Adidas and England Athletics.
  • LIKELYThe easiest opening is a niche they serve badly: coaches who need progress over time, a back-view analysis, or tracking a runner back from injury.
